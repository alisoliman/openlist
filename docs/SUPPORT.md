# Openlist support

Openlist is maintained by Ali Soliman. For help, bug reports or feature requests,
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/alisoliman/openlist/issues).
A GitHub account is required to post.

Include your device model, iOS version, Openlist version from the bottom of
Settings, and the steps that led to the problem. Describe what you expected and
what happened. Screenshots can help, but remove personal information first.
GitHub issues are public; do not post your task library, credentials or sensitive
attachments.

## Common questions

- **iCloud sync:** check **Openlist → Settings → iCloud sync** for status or an
  error. Use the same Apple Account on your devices and check available iCloud
  storage. Sync runs on Apple's schedule and may not be immediate.
- **Voice capture:** check microphone permission in iPhone Settings. Speech
  models may need to download before first use. Turning speech into several
  tasks depends on Apple Intelligence availability for your device and language.
- **Calendar access:** connect calendars from Openlist Settings and allow access
  in iPhone Settings. Openlist reads events to plan around them; it does not edit
  your calendar.
- **Recover a deleted task:** open **Settings → Trash** and tap **Restore**.
  Permanently erased items cannot be restored from Trash.
